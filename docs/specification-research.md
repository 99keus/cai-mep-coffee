# Product specification research — 28 September 2026

All 28 profiles are editable in `data/product-specifications.ts`. They are reference drafts, not verified Cai Mep lot specifications. Change `status` to `confirmed` only after reviewing laboratory/sample/contract requirements. No supplier certifications, capacities, origins, MOQ or lead times are adopted.

Binh Minh Cafe technical-analysis fields were read from its live Specifications tab. Labels and values (including `<` rather than `≤`, ranges, and mixed-screen wording) are retained. Nine corresponding entries were found. Master product names, grades and processes remain unchanged. Arabica S18/S16 Clean reference pages explicitly describe fully washed processing.

There is no universal export specification for every species/grade/process combination. Other supplier values are benchmarks, not legal compliance claims. TCVN catalogue links identify the relevant standards but do not prove compliance or establish all destination requirements. VietFarm's indexed standard text supplies the 5% roasted/ground moisture reference; the PDF/full page timed out during retrieval. Roast level, grind size, blend ratio, crop year and unverified limits are not fabricated.
